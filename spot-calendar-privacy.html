This Privacy Policy explains how Spot Calendar (“the app”), developed by Haponiuk (“we”, “us”), handles your information. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

The short version

Everything stays on your phone

On device

Your activities, templates and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers, and we never see your data.

Read only

The app reads your phone’s calendar to know when you’re busy. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in it.

1Who we are

Spot Calendar is developed by Haponiuk, an independent developer based in Corrientes, Argentina. For any question about this policy or your data, write to ivanhaponiuk01@gmail.com.

2Information the app uses

Spot Calendar uses the following information only to show your schedule and your free time, and only on your device:

  • Your phone’s calendar. If you grant the calendar permission, the app reads the events in the calendars on your phone (titles, dates and times, locations and descriptions) and the name of each calendar and the account it belongs to, which may be an email address. It uses them to display your events and to know when you’re busy. Access is read-only. You can choose which calendars count, use the app without this permission, and revoke it at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • What you create. Your activities, templates, colors, the places and notes you type, your reminders and your settings, stored in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Reminders. If you ask to be reminded before an activity, the app schedules a notification on your device. Reminders never go through a server.
  • Widgets. If you add Spot Calendar widgets, they show your upcoming activities and events on your home screen, where anyone looking at your phone can see them.
  • Premium status. Whether you bought Premium is stored on your device and confirmed with Google Play.

3Information we collect

None. We don’t run servers and we don’t receive any of your data. The app has no user accounts and doesn’t collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. We don’t sell, rent or share personal information with anyone.

4Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC). Premium is purchased through Google Play, using the Google Play Billing Library included in the app. To show prices, process your purchase and confirm it, the library communicates with Google Play, which handles your Google account and payment details. The library also sends Google technical diagnostic information about its own operation. We never receive your payment details. This processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Android backup (Google). If you have turned on your phone’s backup, Android may include Spot Calendar’s data in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is an Android feature that you manage in your phone’s settings, governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Backup files you export. If you use “Guardar una copia” (save a copy), the app creates a file with your activities, templates and settings in the place you choose: your phone, or a storage app you pick. That file is under your control, and how it’s handled depends on where you save it. Restoring a copy reads only the file you select.
  • Your calendar provider. The events in your phone’s calendar are synced by the calendar app or account that holds them (for example, Google Calendar), not by Spot Calendar.

5Permissions

  • Calendar READ_CALENDAR: read your phone’s calendar events to know when you’re busy. Read-only.
  • Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS: show the reminders you ask for.
  • Alarms and reminders USE_EXACT_ALARM (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM on Android 12 and older): deliver each reminder at the exact time.
  • Run at startup RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: reschedule your reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only by the Google Play Billing Library, for the Premium purchase. The app itself doesn’t connect to the internet.
  • Google Play billing com.android.vending.BILLING: buy Premium.

6How your data is protected

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. The app never sends your calendar or anything you create to us or to anyone else. Communication with Google Play for purchases is encrypted in transit by Google. Backup files you export are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

7Data retention and deletion

We don’t retain any of your data, because we never receive it. On your phone, your data stays until you delete it:

  • delete activities and templates inside the app;
  • erase everything in Settings › Apps › Spot Calendar › Storage › Clear data;
  • or uninstall the app, which removes all of its data.

Android backups are managed, and can be deleted, from your phone’s settings and your Google account. Purchase records are kept by Google under its own policy. Files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU and UK GDPR, Argentina’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326, Brazil’s LGPD or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Argentina, the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública).

Because Spot Calendar keeps your data on your device, you can see, change, export and delete it directly in the app and in your phone’s settings. We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use it for automated decision-making or profiling. For any request or question, contact us.

9Children

Spot Calendar is not directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll help.
